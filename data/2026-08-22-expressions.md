# 2026-08-22 표현

### stay away from
#### 의미
~을 피하다, ~에서 멀리하다
#### 사용법
stay away from + 명사(구)
#### 예문
Shark experts are warning influencers to stay away from sharks.

### point out
#### 의미
지적하다, 짚어주다
#### 사용법
point out + 명사(구) / that절
#### 예문
The scientists pointed out hundreds of areas of activity that look like twisters of fire.

### lead to
#### 의미
~로 이어지다, ~을 초래하다
#### 사용법
lead to + 명사(구) / 동명사(-ing)
#### 예문
It led to bad blood between Harry and his family.

### zoom in on
#### 의미
~을 확대해서 보다, 자세히 들여다보다
#### 사용법
zoom in on + 명사(구)
#### 예문
The new images are the closest ever that scientists have zoomed in on the Sun.

### figure out
#### 의미
알아내다, 파악하다
#### 사용법
figure out + 명사(구) / how(what 등) + to부정사
#### 예문
The photos could help us to figure out and eventually predict space weather.
