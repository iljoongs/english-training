## Working from Home

### Text
More and more companies are allowing their employees to work from home at least two days a week. Supporters say this saves time and money because workers do not have to commute. A recent survey found that many workers would rather quit than go back to the office five days a week.

### Words
commute (v) (통근하다) (commute to work)
survey (n) (설문 조사) (a recent survey found that ~)
would rather (phrase) (차라리 ~하겠다) (would rather A than B)

### Writing
commute | 집과 직장 사이를 규칙적으로 오갈 때 | I commute to work by bus every day.
would rather | 두 가지 중 하나를 더 원할 때. would rather A than B | I would rather walk than wait for the bus.

## Sleep and Memory

### Text
Researchers warn that many teenagers do not get enough rest. Phones and computers give off blue light, which makes it difficult to fall asleep.

### Words
give off (phrase) (빛·열 등을 내뿜다) (give off heat)
fall asleep (phrase) (잠들다) (fall asleep on the sofa)

### Writing
fall asleep | I fell asleep during the class.
